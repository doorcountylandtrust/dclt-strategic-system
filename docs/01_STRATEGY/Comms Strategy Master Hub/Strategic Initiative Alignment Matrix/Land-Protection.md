# Land Protection

**Strategic Goal:** Safeguard Door County's most valuable lands and waters by accelerating land protection projects. Use conservation easements, acquisitions, and partnerships to protect 75 new high-priority sites over 5 years.

---

## Messaging

**Primary:**
- "Protect Door County's most important natural places—forever."

**Supporting:**
- "Every acre preserved is a gift to future generations."
- "Your land can outlast you."
- "Conservation is a long game. We're here for the long haul."

**For Landowners:**
- "You hold something irreplaceable."
- "We don't buy land to flip it. We protect it forever."
- "A conservation easement keeps your land in your family—and out of development."

### User Journeys

**Landowner considering options:**
1. Hears about DCLT from neighbor or event
2. Visits website, finds "Protect Your Land"
3. Reads easement explainer, sees testimonial
4. Submits inquiry form or calls
5. Staff begins confidential conversation

**Donor motivated by land protection:**
1. Reads story about new acquisition
2. Sees "Support Our Work" threshold on homepage
3. Clicks through to donate
4. Selects "Land Acquisition Fund" designation (if available)
