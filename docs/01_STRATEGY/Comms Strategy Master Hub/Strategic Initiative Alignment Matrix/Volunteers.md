# Volunteers

**Strategic Goal:** Broaden and diversify the volunteer corps. Strengthen the application of volunteer work in support of DCLT's mission and community engagement. Convert volunteers to members.

---

## Messaging

**Primary:**
- "Volunteers are the heart of Door County Land Trust."

**Supporting:**
- "Together, we care for the lands and waters that make this place home."
- "From restoring trails to leading hikes, volunteers connect people to nature."
- "There's a role for everyone—get involved!"

**Action-Oriented:**
- "Two hours. One morning. Real impact."
- "No experience needed. Just show up."
- "Your Saturday could restore a prairie."

**For Conversion (Volunteer → Member):**
- "You've given your time. Now your gift can keep the work going."
- "You've walked these trails. Help us protect them forever."

### User Journeys

**New volunteer (never engaged):**
1. Sees social post or hears from friend about workday
2. Visits /take-action/volunteer
3. Reads "No experience needed"
4. Browses upcoming opportunities or submits interest form
5. Gets email from staff with next steps
6. Shows up, has good experience
7. Returns, eventually becomes member

**Experienced volunteer (deepen engagement):**
1. Has volunteered 3+ times
2. Receives email: "Want to become a Preserve Steward?"
3. Visits volunteer hub, sees leadership roles
4. Applies for trail steward or hike leader training
5. Takes on recurring responsibility

**Volunteer → Donor conversion:**
1. Volunteers regularly for a season
2. Receives targeted appeal: "You've given your time..."
3. Clicks through to donate page
4. Becomes member

## Gaps & Opportunities

### Content Gaps
- [ ] No volunteer spotlight stories on website
- [ ] No clear role descriptions (Preserve Steward, Hike Leader, etc.)
- [ ] No "What to expect at your first workday" content
- [ ] No photo submission pathway

### UX Gaps
- [ ] Volunteer workdays mixed with all events—hard to filter
- [ ] No way to see "volunteers needed" urgency
- [ ] No volunteer dashboard/portal (future, post-launch)

### Conversion Gaps
- [ ] No automated volunteer → member nurture sequence
- [ ] No tracking of volunteer hours in Supabase (Salesforce only?)
- [ ] No volunteer recognition on website (names, hours, impact)

### Strategic Plan Alignment
- [ ] "Committees have non-board volunteers" — not website-visible
- [ ] "Broader demographics" — imagery and language audit needed
