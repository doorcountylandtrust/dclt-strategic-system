# Conservation Partnerships

**Strategic Goal:** Advance DCLT's mission by strengthening current conservation partnerships and developing new collaboratives. Increase projects done in collaboration with tribes, agencies, universities, and other conservation organizations.

---

## Messaging

**Primary:**
- "Together, we protect more land, water, and wildlife."

**Supporting:**
- "Strong partnerships help us tackle big challenges—climate change, water quality, and habitat loss."
- "Collaborating with tribes, agencies, and conservation leaders multiplies our impact."

**For Partners:**
- "Your expertise. Our mission. Shared impact."
- "Conservation works better together."
