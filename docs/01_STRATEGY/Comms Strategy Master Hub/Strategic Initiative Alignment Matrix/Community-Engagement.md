# Community Engagement

**Strategic Goal:** Connect people to conservation. Raise awareness about how land protection supports health, recreation, and resilience. Respond to local issues like water quality and climate change. Increase DCLT's visibility as a valuable community resource.

---

## Messaging

**Primary:**
- "Protecting the places we love, together."

**Supporting:**
- "When we care for the land, the land takes care of us."
- "Conservation means clean water, fresh air, and natural beauty—for everyone, now and always."

**Climate/Resilience Thread (subtle):**
- "The preserves you love help protect our community from flooding, erosion, and extreme weather."
- "Healthy lands are resilient lands."

**Recreation/Memory:**
- "We're safeguarding the places where memories are made—hiking, birding, paddling, exploring."
- "Your support keeps Door County's landscapes healthy, scenic, and thriving—for generations."

**Community Benefit:**
- "Conservation isn't just about nature. It's about community."
- "Protected lands benefit everyone—whether you hike them or not."

### User Journeys

**Casual visitor discovering DCLT:**
1. Searches "hiking Door County" or "nature preserves near me"
2. Lands on preserve page via search
3. Plans visit, has good experience
4. Returns to website, signs up for newsletter
5. Receives event invitation, attends
6. Becomes member

**Community member seeking connection:**
1. Moved to Door County, wants to meet people
2. Finds DCLT events through social or search
3. Attends guided hike or volunteer workday
4. Meets like-minded people
5. Becomes regular participant, eventually volunteer or donor

**Local leader seeking partnership:**
1. School teacher, civic group leader, or business owner
2. Visits website looking for programming or partnership
3. Finds events, contact info
4. Reaches out to staff
5. Co-hosts event or sponsors program

**Climate-curious resident:**
1. Concerned about flooding, water quality, development
2. Searches for local environmental organizations
3. Finds DCLT, reads about land protection benefits
4. Sees connection between conservation and community resilience
5. Signs up for newsletter, eventually donates

## Content Strategy: Stories from the Land

The "Stories" section should support community engagement with:

| Story Type | Frequency | Purpose |
|------------|-----------|---------|
| Field Note | Monthly | Stewardship updates, seasonal observations |
| Volunteer Spotlight | Quarterly | Recognize people, recruit others |
| Donor Impact | Quarterly | Show what gifts accomplish |
| Preserve Feature | As needed | Deeper dive on specific places |
| Community Partner | As needed | Schools, businesses, civic groups |
| Event Recap | After major events | Photos, quotes, momentum |

This becomes the "Living Time" layer of community connection.
