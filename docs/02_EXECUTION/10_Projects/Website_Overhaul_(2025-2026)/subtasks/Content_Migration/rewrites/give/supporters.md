# GRATITUDE INTRO

## Thank You

Behind every protected forest, shoreline, and meadow is a person who decided it mattered.

Some give annually. Some give monthly. Some make a single gift when the moment feels right. Some include us in their estate plans, thinking decades ahead. Some show up with work gloves on a Saturday morning.

There's no minimum. No test. No required level. Just a shared belief that Door County's wild places are worth protecting — and that we protect them together.

**To everyone who has given, volunteered, or spoken up for conservation: thank you.**

# RECOGNITION NOTE

## Donor Recognition

We gratefully acknowledge our supporters in our print publications throughout the year:

- **Landings** — Our journal, mailed three times per year
- **Annual Report** — A yearly summary of our conservation work and supporters
- **Hiking Maps** — Distributed at preserves across the county

Donors at all levels are recognized. If you prefer to give anonymously, we're happy to honor that — just let us know.

For those who include the Land Trust in their estate plans, we welcome you to the **[Legacy Circle](/give/legacy-giving)** — a community of people whose commitment to Door County will outlast their lifetimes.

# PAGE NOTES

## What This Page Does

- Thanks supporters without creating hierarchy
- Features *why* people give, not *how much*
- Provides pathways to join without pressure
- Acknowledges recognition exists (in print) without building the website around it

## What This Page Doesn't Do

- List donors by tier on the website
- Emphasize exclusivity or status
- Push hard for conversion (that's for /donate and /join-renew)

## Generational Balance

| For older donors | For younger donors |
|------------------|-------------------|
| Recognition mentioned (print) | Focus on impact and community |
| Legacy Circle highlighted | Stories over status |
| Warm, dignified tone | No performative tier structure |
| They see themselves in the quotes | They see themselves in the values |
