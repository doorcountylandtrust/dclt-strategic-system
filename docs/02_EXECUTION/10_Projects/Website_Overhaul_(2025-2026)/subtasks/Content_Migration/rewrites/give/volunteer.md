## This Is How Land Gets Protected

Not in meetings. Not on paper. Out here—hands in the dirt, boots on the trail.

Every spring, volunteers fan out across Door County's preserves to pull garlic mustard before it takes over. In summer, they're clearing brush, counting butterflies, guiding families on nature walks. Come fall, they're planting native seeds that'll bloom for decades.

Some come once. Some come every month. A few have been showing up since we started this work in 1986.

What they share: a love for this place and a willingness to do the work.

We'll partner your skills and interests with a task that needs care. If that sounds like you, we'd be glad to have you.
[/block:text]

### Preserve Workdays
Show up on a Saturday morning, grab some gloves, and spend a few hours pulling invasive plants, clearing trails, or planting natives. You'll work alongside staff and neighbors who know the land—and you'll see real results by the time you leave.

**Time:** 2–4 hours, typically Saturdays
**Perfect for:** First-timers, families (ages 8+), anyone who likes working outdoors
[cta: View Upcoming Workdays → /events?type=workday]

### Trail Stewards
Adopt a trail section at one of our preserves. Walk it regularly, keep an eye out for problems, do light maintenance. A quiet, independent way to give back—on your own schedule.

**Time:** 2–4 hours/month, flexible
**Perfect for:** Regular hikers, retirees, solo volunteers
[cta: Learn About Trail Stewardship → #signup]

### Hike Leading
Guide visitors through our preserves. Lead, co-lead, or assist with group hikes and educational walks. Share what you know about the land—or learn alongside our naturalists.

**Time:** Event-based, 2–4 hours
**Perfect for:** Experienced hikers, educators, people-people
[cta: Become a Hike Leader → #signup]

### Citizen Science
Help track what's happening on the land. Monitor breeding birds, document wildflower bloom times, map invasive species. We'll train you. Your data shapes how we care for these places.

**Time:** Seasonal programs, flexible hours
**Perfect for:** Birders, nature nerds, teachers, detail-oriented folks
[cta: Explore Monitoring Programs → /resources/citizen-science]

### Event & Outreach Support
Help at hikes, educational programs, festivals, and community gatherings. Staff a booth, greet visitors, answer questions, assist with setup. A good way to meet people and represent the Land Trust.

**Time:** Event-based, 2–6 hours
**Perfect for:** Social folks, those curious about DCLT
[cta: See Upcoming Events → /events]

### Families & Youth Groups
Many workdays welcome kids ages 8+ with an adult. It's a chance to teach young people what stewardship feels like. We also partner with scout troops, school groups, and church crews for custom projects.

**Time:** Varies by event
**Perfect for:** Parents, grandparents, teachers, youth leaders
[cta: Plan a Group Visit → /contact]

### Office & Mailing Help
Support our small staff with filing, data entry, mailings, and special projects. Work from our Sturgeon Bay office—especially helpful during busy seasons.

**Time:** Flexible, ongoing
**Perfect for:** Those with office skills, limited mobility, or winter availability
[cta: Contact Us → /contact]

### Share Your Skills
Photographer? GIS mapper? Carpenter? Grant writer? We're a small team and professional skills go a long way. Let us know what you bring.

**Time:** Project-based
**Perfect for:** Professionals wanting to apply expertise
[cta: Tell Us What You Do → #signup]

## Your First Time? Here's What to Know.

**Just show up.** We'll take care of the rest. Tools, gloves, water, instruction—all provided. Wear clothes that can get muddy. Bring sunscreen if it's summer, layers if it's not.

**No commitment required.** Come once and see how it feels. Come back if you want to. We're grateful for whatever time you can spare—no guilt, no pressure.

**You'll actually learn something.** Our staff and longtime volunteers love sharing what they know. You might leave being able to identify five new plants, or understanding why we're removing buckthorn, or knowing where the sandhill cranes nest.

**Kids are welcome at most workdays.** Ages 8 and up can join with an adult. It's muddy, it's fun, and it sticks with them. Check the event listing or reach out if you're unsure.

**Questions? Just ask.** We're neighbors, not a bureaucracy. Email us, call us, or grab someone at a workday. We'll help you find the right fit.
[/block:text]

[block:faq]
title: Questions We Hear a Lot

**Do I need any experience?**
Nope. We'll show you what to do. If you can follow directions and don't mind getting dirty, you're set.

**What if I have physical limits?**
We have roles for every ability—trail monitoring, office help, event greeting, data entry. Tell us what works for you.

**Can I bring my kids? My grandkids?**
Yes, for most workdays (ages 8+ with an adult). Check the event listing or ask us. Grandparents and grandkids make a great team.

**Is there a minimum commitment?**
No. One workday counts. So does fifty.

**Can I get service hours documented?**
Absolutely. We'll sign off on hours for students, court-ordered service, or corporate programs.

**What about group volunteering?**
We love hosting corporate teams, scout troops, school groups, and church crews. Reach out and we'll plan something.

**I'm only here in summer. Can I still help?**
Yes—summer is our busiest season. Get on the list and we'll let you know what's happening while you're here.
[/block:faq]
