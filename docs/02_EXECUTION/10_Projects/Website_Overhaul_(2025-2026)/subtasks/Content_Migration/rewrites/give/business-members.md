# VALUE PROPOSITION

## Good for Business. Good for Door County.

Your customers come to Door County for the forests, shorelines, and wild places. As a Business Member, you help protect what brings people here — and you show your community that you care.

It's a partnership that makes sense: your support funds conservation, and we share your name with thousands of residents and visitors who value what you're protecting.

**Your logo and name reach:**

| Channel | Annual Reach |
|---------|--------------|
| *Landings* journal | 25,000 households (75,000+ views) |
| Hiking Maps | 20,000 distributed county-wide |
| Email Newsletter | 10,000+ subscribers (150,000 potential views) |
| Annual Report | 4,500 supporters |
| Website | 63,000 annual visitors |
| Events & Materials | Thousands more throughout the year |

*These aren't just impressions — they're your neighbors and customers seeing that you invest in the community.*

# BENEFITS DETAIL

## Where You'll Be Seen

### Landings Journal
Our flagship publication reaches 25,000 Door County households three times per year. Business members are prominently listed, with logo display for Silver level and above.

### Hiking Maps
The most-used trail guide in Door County. 20,000 maps distributed annually at preserves, visitor centers, and local businesses. Your logo travels with hikers all season.

### Email Newsletter
Delivered 10–15 times per year to over 10,000 subscribers who care about Door County's environment. That's 150,000+ potential impressions annually.

### Annual Report
Mailed to 4,500 of our most engaged supporters — people who give, volunteer, and advocate for conservation. Your name is seen by the community's most active members.

### Website
Our site receives 63,000 visits per year from people researching preserves, trails, and ways to support conservation. Business members are listed in our footer and on this page.

### Events and More
Throughout the year, business members are recognized at hikes, fundraisers, community events, and in additional print materials.

# PAGE NOTES

## Framing Decisions

**Community, not corporate.** This isn't a CSR program for distant headquarters. It's local businesses supporting local land. The language reflects that:
- "Your neighbors and customers"
- "40+ local businesses"
- "Community" repeated intentionally

**Mutual benefit, not charity.** Businesses get real visibility with real numbers. This is a fair exchange, not just philanthropy.

**Proof of value.** The impression numbers are compelling — include them prominently.

## What This Page Replaces
