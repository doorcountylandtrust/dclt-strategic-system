# LEGACY INTRO

## A Gift That Outlasts a Lifetime

You've spent years enjoying Door County's forests, shorelines, and wild places. A planned gift ensures these landscapes remain protected — not just for your children, but for generations you'll never meet.

Legacy giving isn't about the size of your estate. It's about intention. By including Door County Land Trust in your will or estate plans, you join a community of people who believe some things are worth protecting forever.

*No matter the size of your gift, we can help you create a legacy that lasts.*

# GIVING OPTIONS

## Ways to Leave a Legacy

There are many ways to include Door County Land Trust in your estate plans. The right choice depends on your circumstances, assets, and goals. We recommend consulting with your attorney or financial advisor.

<!-- Accordion block — each type expands for detail -->

### Bequest in Your Will or Trust

The simplest way to leave a legacy. You designate a specific amount, a percentage of your estate, or what remains after other gifts to family.

**Types of bequests:**

- **Specific bequest:** A defined dollar amount or specific asset
- **Percentage bequest:** A portion of your total estate (e.g., 10%)
- **Residuary bequest:** What remains after other beneficiaries receive their gifts
- **Contingent bequest:** Takes effect only if primary beneficiaries are unable to inherit

**Tax benefit:** The value of your bequest is deducted from your taxable estate with no limit on the deduction amount.

---

### Retirement Plan Beneficiary

Name Door County Land Trust as a beneficiary of your IRA, 401(k), or other retirement account. This is one of the most tax-efficient legacy gifts.

**Why it works:** Retirement assets left to individuals are subject to both estate and income taxes — beneficiaries may receive less than 40% of the value. Assets left to a nonprofit pass tax-free in full.

**How to do it:**

1. Contact your plan administrator or log into your account
2. Update your beneficiary designation form
3. You can designate DCLT as primary, secondary, or partial beneficiary
4. Notify us so we can thank you and welcome you to the Legacy Circle

**Qualified Charitable Distributions (QCDs):** If you're 70½ or older, you can also make tax-free gifts directly from your IRA during your lifetime (up to $100,000/year).

<!--

---

### Life Insurance

Name Door County Land Trust as a beneficiary of a life insurance policy, or donate a policy you no longer need.

**Options:**

- **Beneficiary designation:** Name DCLT as primary or contingent beneficiary. The death benefit passes to us tax-free.
- **Policy donation:** Transfer ownership of a paid-up policy you no longer need. You receive a tax deduction, and we receive the benefit.

---

### Charitable Remainder Trust

A trust that provides you (or loved ones) with income during your lifetime, with the remainder going to Door County Land Trust.

**Benefits:**

- Immediate income tax deduction
- Potential to avoid capital gains on appreciated assets
- Steady income stream for life or a term of years
- Meaningful gift to conservation

**Types:**

- **Charitable Remainder Unitrust:** Payments vary based on trust value (good if you expect growth)
- **Charitable Remainder Annuity Trust:** Fixed payments regardless of trust performance (good for predictable income)

*This is a complex instrument. We recommend working with your financial advisor and attorney.*

---

### Charitable Lead Trust

The reverse of a remainder trust — Door County Land Trust receives payments during the trust term, and the remainder passes to your heirs.

**Benefits:**

- Reduce gift or estate taxes on assets transferred to heirs
- Support conservation now while preserving wealth for family
- Works well with assets expected to appreciate

*Consult your estate planning attorney to determine if this is right for your situation.*

---

### Charitable Gift Annuity

Make a gift of $10,000 or more in cash or securities and receive fixed payments for life in return.

**Benefits:**

- Guaranteed income for life (rates based on age)
- Immediate income tax deduction
- Portion of payments may be tax-free
- Remainder supports conservation after your lifetime

**Options:**

- **Immediate annuity:** Payments begin right away
- **Deferred annuity:** Payments begin at a future date (higher rates)

# SAMPLE LANGUAGE

## Sample Bequest Language

If you're working with an estate attorney, the following language can help direct your gift:

### Unrestricted Gift

> I give to Door County Land Trust, Inc., a nonprofit corporation located in Sturgeon Bay, Wisconsin (Tax ID 39-1561423), [the sum of $_______ / _______ percent of my estate / the rest, residue, and remainder of my estate] to be used for its general charitable purposes.

### Gift to the Stewardship Endowment

> I give to Door County Land Trust, Inc., a nonprofit corporation located in Sturgeon Bay, Wisconsin (Tax ID 39-1561423), [the sum of $_______ / _______ percent of my estate] to be added to the Stewardship Endowment Fund, ensuring funds are always available to care for protected lands.

### Gift to the Land Acquisition Fund

> I give to Door County Land Trust, Inc., a nonprofit corporation located in Sturgeon Bay, Wisconsin (Tax ID 39-1561423), [the sum of $_______ / _______ percent of my estate] to be added to the Land Acquisition Fund, providing resources to protect properties of high ecological and scenic value.
