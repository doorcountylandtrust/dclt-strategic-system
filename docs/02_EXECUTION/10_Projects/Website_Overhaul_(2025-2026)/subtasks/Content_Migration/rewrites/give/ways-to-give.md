# STOCK GIFTS

<!-- Anchor: #stock-gifts -->

## Give Stock or Securities

Donating appreciated stock, bonds, or mutual funds is one of the most tax-efficient ways to support conservation. You receive a charitable deduction for the full market value while avoiding capital gains tax on the appreciation.

### How It Works

1. **Contact us first** — Call (920) 746-1359 or email giving@doorcountylandtrust.org to let us know your gift is coming
2. **Instruct your broker** — Transfer shares to our account using the details below
3. **We'll acknowledge your gift** — You'll receive a tax receipt for the full market value on the date of transfer

### Transfer Instructions

Provide your broker with the following information:

| Detail | Information |
|--------|-------------|
| **Receiving Firm** | Charles Schwab & Co., Inc. |
| **DTC Number** | 0164 |
| **Account Number** | 6509-8144 |
| **Account Name** | Door County Land Trust |
| **Tax ID** | 39-1561423 |

### Sample Letter to Your Broker

> Please transfer ______ shares of ______________ stock to the benefit of Door County Land Trust's account at Charles Schwab (DTC #0164) to the credit of account number 6509-8144.

**Important:** Electronic transfers don't include donor information. Please notify us so we can properly acknowledge your gift and provide your tax receipt.

### Questions?

Contact Cinnamon Rossman, Director of Charitable Giving  
(920) 746-1359 · giving@doorcountylandtrust.org

# DONOR-ADVISED FUNDS

<!-- Anchor: #donor-advised-funds -->

## Give from Your Donor-Advised Fund

If you have a donor-advised fund (DAF), you can recommend a grant to Door County Land Trust. This is a simple way to support conservation using funds you've already set aside for charitable giving.

### Our Information

| Detail | Information |
|--------|-------------|
| **Legal Name** | Door County Land Trust, Inc. |
| **Address** | P.O. Box 65, Sturgeon Bay, WI 54235 |
| **Tax ID (EIN)** | 39-1561423 |

### How to Give

1. Log into your DAF account (Fidelity Charitable, Schwab Charitable, Vanguard Charitable, or your community foundation)
2. Search for "Door County Land Trust" or use our Tax ID above
3. Recommend a grant amount
4. Let us know it's coming so we can thank you properly

**Tip:** Some DAF platforms allow recurring grants — a great way to provide sustained support.

# MATCHING GIFTS

<!-- Anchor: #matching-gifts -->

## Double Your Impact

Many employers match charitable gifts made by their employees. Check if your company offers a matching gift program — you could double or even triple your donation to Door County Land Trust.

**How to check:**
1. Ask your HR department if your company has a matching gift program
2. Request a matching gift form (paper or online)
3. Submit the form with your donation details
4. We'll confirm your gift, and your employer sends their match
