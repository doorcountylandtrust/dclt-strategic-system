Paige Witek - Community Conservation Coordinator



**Meeting Purpose:** Align on goals for the Sign up section of the new website — what's working, what's not, and what we want to build.

**Time:** 45–60 minutes

### 1. Current State (10 min)

**For you (Coordinator):**

- What's frustrating about the current event setup?
- What takes more time than it should?
- What questions do you get from visitors that the website doesn't answer?

**For me (Developer):**

- Pain point: Creating events twice (website + Give Lively)
- The events plugin feels limiting and prescriptive
- Design feels dated

### 2. Your 2026 Programming Plans (10 min)

- What types of events are you planning? (Workdays, guided hikes, educational programs, fundraisers, etc.)
- Any new recurring programs or series?
- Seasonal patterns we should design for?
- Are there events that need different handling? (Paid vs. free, registration required vs. drop-in, family-friendly, etc.)

### 3. User Experience (10 min)

How should visitors find events?

- By date? By type? By preserve location? By audience (families, etc.)?
- What info do people need at a glance vs. on the detail page?
- What's the ideal signup flow?

### 4. Features Wishlist (10 min)

What would make your life easier — and make the visitor experience better?

_Some possibilities to react to:_

- Filtering by event type, date, location
    
- Automated waitlists
- Single system for events + registration (no double entry)
- Recurring events that auto-generate
- Calendar view + list view
- Integration with email (auto-confirmation, reminders)
- Capacity tracking / "Sold Out" indicators
- Family-friendly or accessibility tags
